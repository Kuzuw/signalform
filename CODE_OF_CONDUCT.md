# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Signalform to be a welcoming project for everyone, whatever their age, body, ability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behavior that helps:

- being kind and patient, especially with newcomers;
- respecting different opinions, experiences and ways of working;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully;
- taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them;
- focusing on what is best for the community and the project.

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- trolling, insults or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing other people's private information without their permission;
- any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews) and when someone represents the project elsewhere.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainer privately, through the contact details on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Kuzuw). Reports are reviewed promptly and kept confidential.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful.
